Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This page explains how HABrowser handles information. The site and relay are operated by Segually. Contact segual@segually.dev with privacy questions or requests.

Local saves and backups

Your local game files, including saves and simulated purchase records, are stored in your browser's IndexedDB on your device. Our save import and export tools process those files locally. They do not upload your files or ZIP backups to our server.

The general_ file includes login information and simulated purchase state. An exported backup may therefore contain sensitive account information. Keep it private. You can delete local game data through your browser's site-data settings; clearing it can permanently remove saves unless you have a backup.

Local storage is separate from multiplayer communications: logging in, chatting, joining a server, and playing online requires sending the relevant account and gameplay information to other servers.

Multiplayer activity logs

We keep server-side logs that associate your multiplayer activity with your IP address and multiplayer account name. These logs are used for analytical purposes, including understanding connection activity and server usage, and for diagnosing connection problems and protecting the relay against abuse.

Depending on the connection, records include:

  • Your IP address, connection identifier, and multiplayer account name when available.
  • Connection, authentication, and disconnection timestamps.
  • The destination server's IP address, port, and name when available.
  • Server join and leave timestamps, and connection duration.

The activity logger does not store passwords, login codes, secret join codes, authentication tokens, chat contents, or complete gameplay packets. Separate technical server logs may record requests, connection errors, and diagnostic information.

Logs are stored on the server and are not published as a player directory. The current logging system does not automatically delete old logs; records remain until manually deleted. There is currently no fixed deletion schedule. You can contact us to request deletion of information associated with you, subject to any applicable obligations or need to retain information for a security investigation.

Traffic forwarded to community servers

Our relay forwards multiplayer traffic to the community friend server and to game servers authorized by its responses. Those servers receive the account, authentication, chat, and gameplay information needed for multiplayer and may keep their own logs.

The friend server was not created by and is not operated by Abstract Software Inc. HABrowser's operator is not affiliated with its operators. Independent server operators control their own processing and moderation; this policy describes HABrowser's handling of information, not theirs.

No ads and possible Unity telemetry

HABrowser has no ads. We do not add Google Analytics or advertising trackers to the site.

The game was made with Unity. Depending on the Unity components and services active in the game, Unity may collect additional telemetry, such as device or browser information, IP addresses, technical diagnostics, and usage information. Using Unity does not by itself establish that every telemetry service is active in this browser build.

For information about Unity's own processing and available choices, see Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Other disclosures and external links

We do not sell relay activity logs or use them for advertising. Information may be accessed by the operator and service providers supporting hosting, or disclosed where required by law or necessary to address security incidents.

Following links to GitHub, Discord, Reddit, Abstract Software, or Unity takes you to independently operated services with their own privacy policies. Contacting us by email also shares the information you include in your message with us and the email providers involved.

Your choices and requests

You can play offline without connecting to multiplayer servers, manage or delete local storage using your browser, and export your own saves. Deleting browser data does not delete existing server-side logs.

Contact us to ask about information we hold about you or request access, correction, or deletion. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to object to processing, restrict it, receive a portable copy, and complain to a data protection authority. We may need proportionate information to verify that a request concerns your data; do not send passwords or private backup files.

Policy updates

Changes will be published here with an updated date. Material changes will also be announced on the homepage before they take effect.
