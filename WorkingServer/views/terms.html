Simulated purchases

All in-game purchases in this browser port are simulated. They do not charge real money, process payments, or create a real purchase. Simulated purchases do not establish paid entitlements in the official mobile game or any other version.

Your local data

Your local save files and simulated purchase records are stored on your device in your browser. HABrowser does not upload those files: exporting and importing backups happens locally, and the ZIP files are not sent to our server.

This applies to local storage and backup files. Multiplayer necessarily sends account, authentication, and gameplay information through the relay to the servers you use, and our relay keeps connection and activity logs as described in the Privacy Policy.

Clearing browser data, using private browsing, changing browsers, or an update may make local data unavailable. Keep your own backups. Importing replaces matching local files. Backups containing the general_ file include login information and simulated purchase state; keep them private and only import files you trust.
