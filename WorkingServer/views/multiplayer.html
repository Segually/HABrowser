Hybrid Animals Multiplayer Guide

You can play Hybrid Animals for free in your browser by visiting ha.segually.dev. HABrowser connects this unofficial browser port to community multiplayer servers. Here are answers to common questions about playing online.

Updated: 10 October 2026

How do I play multiplayer in Hybrid Animals?

Open HABrowser, select Play, and use the in-game friends and multiplayer menus to sign in or create a multiplayer account. Add your friends using their account names. When a friend is online and their world is available to join, use the join option beside their name. You can also open the Public Servers page from the top bar and select an available community server.

You need an internet connection for multiplayer. Your friend needs to be online and hosting an available world for you to join them. Follow the rules of the server you choose.

How do I play multiplayer after the servers shut down?

If the official multiplayer service you previously used is unavailable, HABrowser offers a separate community route through a privately operated friend server. It does not restore the official service, and old accounts or worlds are not guaranteed to transfer.

You can play Hybrid Animals for free in your browser at ha.segually.dev and use the multiplayer menus to connect to the community service while it is available. The friend server is not created or operated by Abstract Software Inc., and HABrowser is not affiliated with its operators.

How do I report inappropriate content I saw during online play?

Leave the server if you do not want to see its content. Contact that server's owner or moderators through their published contact channel, if they have one. Include the server name, approximate time, player name where relevant, and a brief description. Do not share passwords, login codes, or private save backups.

For abuse involving the HABrowser site or relay, email segual@segually.dev. We can review issues affecting our service, but cannot promise to remove builds or moderate chat on independently operated servers. Community servers may contain inappropriate builds and uncensored or unmoderated chat; this is not content selected or endorsed by HABrowser.

What are the best ways to engage with friends in Hybrid Animals multiplayer?

  • Agree on a server or hosted world and a time to meet.
  • Build a shared settlement, explore together, or gather materials for a crafting project.
  • Ask before changing another player's builds or taking their items.
  • Follow the host's rules and keep account details private.

For the modding community, visit HAModLoader on Discord or r/HAModLoader.

Why can't I connect to a friend or public server?

Check your internet connection, whether the friend is still hosting, and whether the server still appears in the current list. Return to the multiplayer menu and try again rather than reusing an old invitation. A server may be full, offline, incompatible, or temporarily unreachable. The relay permits destinations announced by the community service; it does not accept arbitrary server addresses.

What information is recorded when I play online?

The relay logs IP addresses, associated multiplayer account names when available, and connection and server join/leave timestamps. Multiplayer traffic is forwarded to independent servers. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
