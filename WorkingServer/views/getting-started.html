How to Start Playing Hybrid Animals in Your Browser

Play Hybrid Animals for free in your browser by visiting ha.segually.dev. HABrowser is an unofficial browser port of the mobile game, with local saves and access to community multiplayer.

Updated: 10 October 2026

1. Open the game

Visit the homepage and select Play. Wait for the initial game files to download and load. No app installation is required, but the browser still downloads the files needed to run the game. A stable connection helps with the first load.

2. Create your hybrid and enter a world

Use the game's creature creation screen to choose two animals and combine them into your hybrid. Follow the in-game prompts to start your world. Different animal combinations give your creature a different appearance and abilities.

3. Explore and gather supplies

Start near familiar areas, collect materials, and learn the inventory and crafting menus. Explore gradually instead of rushing into dangerous fights. Our survival and crafting guide explains crafting stations, mutations, and useful first steps.

4. Choose solo or multiplayer play

You can explore your own world or use the friends and Public Servers menus to play online. Multiplayer uses community infrastructure and depends on the selected server being available. See the multiplayer guide for joining friends, connection troubleshooting, and reporting concerns.

5. Back up your progress

Progress is saved locally in your browser, rather than uploaded as a cloud backup by HABrowser. On the homepage, use Export ZIP to save all game data or only the general_ file, which includes login information and simulated purchases. Close all game tabs before importing or exporting.

Import ZIP restores matching files and replaces existing matching data. Keep backups private and make a backup before importing. Clearing browser site data or using temporary browsing storage can remove progress.

Can I play Hybrid Animals on a phone?

The browser port supports touch input and native browser text fields for entering text. Performance depends on your device and browser. If a text entry dialog opens, tap its field to bring up the keyboard, then use Done to confirm. Closing unused tabs can help devices with limited memory.

Do I need to pay to play?

No. You can play Hybrid Animals for free in your browser at ha.segually.dev. This browser port has no ads, and its in-game purchases are simulated: they do not charge money or grant paid entitlements in the official mobile game.
